# JSS Client Questions

**Prepared for:** JSS Mahavidyapeetha / JSS Publications  
**Prepared by:** Brandex  
**Date:** 17 September 2026  
**Purpose:** Clarify requirements for proposed e-commerce website for book publications

---

## General / Business

1. Is JSS Book House currently operational as a retail outlet? If so, what is its official address, phone number, and opening hours?
2. What is the official contact person/department for book sales and publications enquiries?
3. Are all 49 books currently listed on the website available for purchase right now?
4. Are there any books that are out of print, temporarily unavailable, or discontinued?
5. Are there new/unpublished books that should be included in the e-commerce catalogue but are not yet on the website?
6. What is the total number of books in the JSS Granthamale catalogue (the website says "over 100 titles")? How many are currently active for sale?
7. Do you have a catalog of books that are published by other publishers but sold through JSS Book House (as mentioned on the Publications page: "other good titles published by other publishers")? Should these be included in the e-commerce site?

## Inventory & Pricing

8. Which books are currently in stock? Is there an inventory list available?
9. Are the prices displayed on the website current and accurate? When were they last updated?
10. For books with multiple pricing tiers (Ordinary / Special / Deluxe), what is the physical difference between variants? (e.g., binding quality, page quality, special cover, inclusion of additional content)
11. Are all Ordinary/Special/Deluxe variants currently available for all applicable books, or are some variants discontinued?
12. For the Sharana Samskruti Male series (69+ books planned, 69 published so far), what is the full list of published titles? The website currently only lists 6 books in this series — are there more that should be on the site?
13. For the Vachana Vyakyana Male series, what is the complete list of published books? The website shows a subset.
14. Do you have a structured spreadsheet or database of all books with complete metadata (title, author, translator, language, series, category, edition, pages, price, stock status, cover image)?
15. Do ISBNs exist for any of the books? If so, can you provide them?

## Images & Content

16. Do you have higher-resolution original book cover images (beyond the thumbnails currently on the website)? If so, in what format and where are they stored?
17. Do you have descriptions for each book in English, Kannada, or both? The current website has inconsistent descriptions — some are in Kannada only, some in English only.
18. Is there author/translator metadata available for all books in a structured form? (Many books currently have no author listed on their detail pages.)
19. Do you have author biographies that can be used for author profile pages?
20. Are there any sample pages, excerpts, or PDFs that can be provided as previews for potential buyers?
21. For periodical back issues (Prasada Archives), are these intended to be sold as downloads, physical copies, or both?

## Shipping & Fulfillment

22. Do you currently ship books? If so, within Karnataka only, across India, or internationally?
23. What are the shipping charges? Is there free shipping above a certain order value?
24. What is the expected order fulfillment time (days from order to dispatch)?
25. Do you use a specific courier/service for shipping?
26. Do you ship to PO boxes or remote areas?
27. What are the packaging requirements for books (individual wrapping, bulk packing)?

## Payments

28. Do you currently accept online payments? If so, through which system/gateway?
29. Would you like to accept online payments on the new e-commerce site? If yes, which payment methods should be supported? (Credit/debit cards, UPI, net banking, wallets, etc.)
30. Do you want to offer Cash on Delivery (COD)? If yes, are there regions where COD is not available?
31. What is your preferred payment gateway provider? (Razorpay, PayU, Stripe, Cashfree, etc. — or do you already have a merchant account?)
32. Are books subject to GST? If so, what is the GST rate applicable? Books in India may be exempt or have a reduced rate — please confirm.
33. Do you need to generate GST-compliant invoices for each order?
34. Do you need to collect and store customer payment information? (If using a payment gateway, this is typically handled by the gateway.)

## Returns, Refunds, Cancellation, Replacement

35. What is your return policy for books? (Time window, condition requirements, who bears return shipping cost)
36. Do you offer refunds? Under what conditions?
37. What is your cancellation policy? Can customers cancel after placing an order? Before payment? After payment? After dispatch?
38. Do you offer book replacement (e.g., if a book arrives damaged)? What is the process?
39. Are there any books that are non-returnable/non-refundable (e.g., downloadable content)?

## Customer Experience & Features

40. Do you want customers to be able to create accounts on the e-commerce site? (Accounts enable order history, saved addresses, faster checkout.)
41. If accounts are enabled, do you want customers to be able to track their orders online?
42. Do you want to send order confirmation emails to customers? If yes, what should the email contain?
43. Do you want to send shipping/delivery update emails or SMS/WhatsApp messages?
44. Do you want a WhatsApp-based ordering or notification system? (WhatsApp share buttons already exist on the current site.)
45. Do you want a wishlist feature where customers can save books for later purchase?
46. Do you want customers to be able to write reviews/ratings for books? (Not currently on the site.)
47. Do you want to display customer testimonials or endorsements on the site?
48. Do you want a blog/news section for announcing new books, events, or publication news?
49. Do you want the site to be bilingual (Kannada + English) or multilingual?

## Admin & Operations

50. Who will manage the e-commerce website admin? (Add/edit books, update prices, manage inventory, process orders, handle customer enquiries)
51. Does the admin team need training on the e-commerce platform?
52. Do you want the e-commerce site to be a separate website (e.g., books.jssonline.org or shop.jssonline.org) or integrated into the existing jssonline.org website?
53. If integrated into the existing WordPress site, can the current WordPress admin be used for managing book content, or do you want a separate admin interface?
54. Who will be responsible for uploading new books and updating book information?
55. Who will be responsible for processing orders and coordinating shipping?
56. Do you have an existing logo, brand guidelines, or design preferences for the e-commerce site?
57. Do you have existing social media accounts (Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, Twitter/X) for JSS Publications that should be linked from the e-commerce site?
58. Do you have a preferred hosting provider or server infrastructure?

## Legal & Compliance

59. Do you have a privacy policy and terms of service that should be displayed on the e-commerce site?
60. Do you collect any personal data from customers that requires compliance with India's data protection regulations (DPDP Act)?
61. Are there any restrictions on who can purchase certain books (e.g., age restrictions, institutional vs. individual buyers)?

## Timeline & Budget

62. What is your expected timeline for launching the e-commerce site?
63. Is there a budget range for this project?
64. Are there any upcoming events (e.g., book fairs, anniversaries, festivals) that the site should be ready for?

---

*These questions are based on research conducted on 17 September 2026 from the public website at https://jssonline.org/publications/books-published and related pages. They represent gaps identified between the current website's capabilities and a fully functional e-commerce platform.*
