## Types / Categories / Series of Publications

### Major Series Identified

1. **Sharana Samskruti Male** (ಶರಣ ಸಂಸ್ಕøತಿ ಮಾಲೆ) — Series of ~100 books introducing Sharana literature, life philosophy, religious and cultural traditions to students and general public. 69 books published so far (as stated on individual book pages). Price range: Rs.12–Rs.500. Target: school/college students and general public.

2. **Vachana Vyakyana Male** (ವಚನ ವ್ಯಾಕರಣ ಮಾಲೆ) — Vachana commentary series. Includes commentaries on Basavanna, Allama Prabhu, Channabasavanna, Ganalinga Deva, Akkamahadevi, and various Sharanara Vachanagalu editions ( Editions 1, 4, 10, 11 by Dr. Jayashree). Also includes "Virashaiva Punya Purusharu Male" sub-series profiling prominent Virashaiva personalities.

3. **Golden Jubilee Endowment Series** — English books (No. 7: Space Technology and Societal Applications; No. 9: Globalization: Challenges and the Need for Co-operation).

4. **Prasada Archives** — Digitized back issues of the Prasada periodical (2006–2026+).

5. **Audio/Video CD Series** — Vachana Sangama (Vols 1–7), Nivedane (Vols 1–3), Divya Chetana (Vols 1–2).

**Source:** https://jssonline.org/publications/books-published/ (individual book detail pages)

## Business Context for Proposed E-Commerce Website

**Key understanding:** JSS Publications / JSS Granthamale is a non-profit / institutional publication division of JSS Mahavidyapeetha, a large educational and social service organization. Their publications are primarily:

- **Kannada-language** (majority) with **some English and regional language** (Tamil, Telugu) titles
- **Spiritual / religious / cultural** content centered on Veerashaiva/Lingayat tradition and Vachana literature
- **Academic and educational** works
- **Low-priced** mass-market books (Rs.10–Rs.500 for most; some premium at Rs.1000–Rs.2000)
- **Multiple pricing tiers:** Ordinary / Special / Deluxe for some books
- **Multiple editions** of the same title (e.g., Sharanara Vachanagalu Editions 4, 10, 11)
- **Series-based** catalog (Sharana Samskruti Male, Vachana Vyakyana Male)

**The current website is NOT an e-commerce site.** It is a brochure/display site. Books are listed with cover images, prices, and page counts, but **no buying mechanism exists on the site**.

## Potential Website Architecture

### Option A: WordPress + WooCommerce (lowest migration friction)

- **Pros:** Client already uses WordPress; JSS staff may be familiar with WP admin; WooCommerce provides cart, checkout, payments, order management out of the box; large ecosystem of plugins; can keep existing content/pages and add shop functionality
- **Cons:** WordPress + WooCommerce can be heavy; may need custom theme work to match the institutional look; performance tuning needed; security updates required
- **Best if:** Client wants to stay on WordPress and have a familiar admin interface

### Option B: Custom e-commerce site (separate from WordPress)

- **Pros:** Clean architecture; purpose-built for book sales; better performance; can be designed to match institutional brand precisely
- **Cons:** Higher development cost; content migration needed from WordPress; separate CMS for non-shop pages
- **Best if:** Client wants a dedicated, high-quality e-commerce experience and is open to a new platform

### Option C: WordPress + headless/custom front-end

- **Pros:** Use WordPress as CMS for book content, custom front-end for shop
- **Cons:** More complex; needs API development
- **Best if:** Client has specific design/UX requirements that WordPress themes can't meet

### Recommended approach (to be confirmed with client):
Given that the client already has a WordPress site with content, **WordPress + WooCommerce** is likely the most pragmatic starting point, with a custom theme or child theme to match the JSS brand. The book catalogue (49 books with metadata) can be migrated into WooCommerce products with custom taxonomies for Series, Category, Language, and Author.

## Suggested Admin Requirements

1. **Product management:** Add/edit books with fields: title, subtitle, author, translator, language, series, category, edition, variant, price (ordinary/special/deluxe), pages, description, cover image, stock status, ISBN (if available)
2. **Series management:** Hierarchical series taxonomy with descriptions; ability to add books to series
3. **Category management:** Category taxonomy aligned with content themes
4. **Author management:** Author profiles with bio; link books to authors
5. **Inventory management:** Stock count per book/SKU; in-stock/out-of-stock toggle; low-stock alerts
6. **Order management:** View orders; update status (pending, processing, shipped, delivered, cancelled); print/send invoices; view order history
7. **Customer management:** View customer list; view customer order history; ban/suspend if needed
8. **Pricing management:** Update prices centrally; manage variant pricing; seasonal discounts
9. **Discount/coupon system:** Optional — create coupon codes, percentage/fixed discounts, expiry
10. **Shipping management:** Configure shipping zones (India / international), rates (flat rate, weight-based, free shipping threshold), COD availability per zone
11. **Content management:** Blog/news section for new book announcements; update institutional pages
12. **Reporting:** Sales reports; top-selling books; inventory reports; customer reports
13. **Notifications:** Configure email templates for order confirmation, shipping updates; optionally WhatsApp/SMS integration
14. **Media management:** Upload and manage high-resolution book cover images; alt text for accessibility and SEO

## Suggested Customer Journey

1. **Discover:** Visitor lands on homepage or comes from search/social; sees featured books, new arrivals, series highlights
2. **Browse:** Visitor explores catalogue by series, category, language, or author; uses search and filters
3. **Evaluate:** Visitor clicks a book → sees detail page with cover, description, metadata, pricing tiers, related books
4. **Add to Cart:** Visitor selects variant (if applicable) and quantity → adds to cart
5. **Review Cart:** Visitor reviews cart, updates quantities, applies coupon if available
6. **Checkout:** Visitor enters/shipping address, selects shipping method, chooses payment (online/COD)
7. **Payment:** Visitor completes payment via gateway or selects COD
8. **Confirmation:** Order confirmation page + email; order ID provided
9. **Fulfillment:** Admin processes order, ships book, updates status
10. **Tracking:** Customer can track order status (if account) or via order ID (if guest)
11. **Post-purchase:** Delivery confirmation; optionally review/rate the book (future feature)
